Introduction
	Develop an end-to-end ANN-based customer response prediction framework to predict term-deposit subscriptions using the UCI Bank Marketing Dataset, enabling targeted marketing, improved customer acquisition, reduced campaign costs, and data-driven business decisions.

Problem Statement (Recommended)
	Traditional bank marketing campaigns often rely on untargeted customer outreach, resulting in low conversion rates, inefficient resource utilization, and increased operational costs. This project develops an end-to-end ANN-based customer response prediction framework using the UCI Bank Marketing Dataset to identify customers most likely to subscribe to term deposits, enabling data-driven customer targeting, improved campaign effectiveness, and higher marketing ROI.
